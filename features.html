Why You Should Trust Astra

Astra uses advanced AI-powered safety features to ensure you're protected at all times.

AI-Powered Motion Detection

Astra's smart AI detects distress based on movement, like running or shaking, and instantly alerts your contacts.

Whisper Alert

Can’t call for help? Just whisper "Help" or a custom phrase, and Astra will recognize your voice and trigger an alert.

Emergency Alert with Volume Button

No internet? No problem! Press your volume button three times, and Astra will notify your trusted contacts with your last known location.

Offline Safety Features

Even without GPS, Astra shares your last known location with your contacts to keep you connected.

Real-Time Location Tracking

Astra predicts safe vs risky areas and sends live location updates to your trusted contacts.

Gesture-Based Alert

Trigger an alert with a simple wrist movement, like clenching your fist or tapping your wrist, using smartwatches or fitness bands.
